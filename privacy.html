Privacy Policy

Annals: History & Its People

Effective November 2026

Thank you for using Annals. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app — the short version: nothing is collected during normal use; the only information we store is what you choose to send through the optional feedback form.

Information We Collect

No account data. The app creates no account of any kind — no sign-up, no sign-in, and no anonymous identifiers. It never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.

Favorites. The items you save as favorites are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers.

Feedback (optional). The feedback form is completely optional and does not affect any app functionality. If you choose to provide it, we collect your email address only to reply to you, your feedback content, app version and device model for diagnosing issues, and a device identifier (IDFV) stored with the feedback record to help diagnose issues.

How We Use It

The feedback information above is used solely to address your feedback (app functionality); it is not used for advertising tracking, profiling, or analytics of any kind, and is not shared with third parties. Subscription receipts are verified with Apple in real time and immediately discarded — no purchase or transaction data is stored or logged.

Third-Party Services

The app is backed by Supabase, which hosts our backend and delivers app content over HTTPS; optional feedback submissions are stored there. It processes this information only to run this service on our behalf.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or trackers. Detail pages may include optional Wikipedia source links that open in your browser — these are not required to use any feature, and Wikipedia handles that browsing under its own privacy policy.

Data Deletion

The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete and no deletion entry is offered. If you would like the feedback you submitted to be removed — or have any other request — email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. No account or device identifiers are created during normal use; if feedback is voluntarily submitted, its deletion can be requested by email as described above.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before or at the time of the relevant app release, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com.
