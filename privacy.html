Privacy Policy

Annals: History & Its People — Effective November 2026

Annals does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Annals works completely offline. The app does not connect to the internet and cannot see any information about you.

No Third Parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, scripts, or services that could receive information.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app does not gather information from children or any other users.

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before or at the time of the relevant app release, and the effective date above will be revised.
