Privacy Policy

Annals: History & Its People

Effective November 2026

Thank you for using Annals. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app — the short version: no account information or usage data is collected during normal use; the only information we store is what you choose to send through the optional feedback form.

Information We Collect

No account data. The app creates no account of any kind — no sign-up, no sign-in — and creates no identifiers during normal use. It never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.

Favorites. The items you save as favorites are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers.

Feedback (optional). The feedback form is completely optional and does not affect any app functionality. If you choose to provide it, we collect your email address only to reply to you, your feedback content, and your app version, device model, and device identifier (IDFV) stored with the feedback record — used together to diagnose issues. This information is kept only to address your feedback; you can request its deletion at any time (see Data Deletion below).

How We Use It

The feedback information above is used solely to address your feedback (app functionality); it is not used for advertising tracking, profiling, or analytics of any kind, and is not shared with anyone except the service providers who process it on our behalf (see Third-Party Services below). Subscription receipts are verified with Apple in real time and immediately discarded — no purchase or transaction data is stored or logged. Purchases and payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase as a service provider to host our backend, deliver app content over HTTPS, and store optional feedback submissions. It processes this information only on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or trackers. Detail pages may include optional Wikipedia source links that open in your browser — these are not required to use any feature, and Wikipedia handles that browsing under its own privacy policy.

Data Deletion

Because the app has no accounts, there is no in-app account deletion. To request deletion of feedback you have submitted — or make any other request — email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Children

The app collects no personal information during normal use from anyone, including children, and is not directed at children under 13. The only exception is feedback you choose to submit (see above); its email address and other details are used only to respond, and if we learn it was submitted by a child we will delete it on request as described above.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before or at the time of the relevant app release, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the feedback information described above.
