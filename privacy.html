Privacy Policy

Annals: History & Its People

Effective November 2026

Thank you for using Annals. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app — the short version: only an anonymous identifier needed to download content, and nothing personal.

Information We Collect

An anonymous user ID. The first time the app launches, it automatically creates an anonymous account with our backend provider so it can download the app's content. This is a random identifier generated by the system — it is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information, and the app never asks you for any.

Favorites. The items you save as favorites are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers.

How We Use It

The anonymous ID is used for exactly two things: authenticating the app's requests so it can download its historical content (figures, events, and civilizations), and identifying the account if you choose to delete it. We do not use it for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The app is backed by Supabase, which hosts our backend and provides anonymous sign-in and content delivery over HTTPS. It processes the anonymous ID only to run this service on our behalf.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or trackers. Detail pages may include optional Wikipedia source links that open in your browser — these are not required to use any feature, and Wikipedia handles that browsing under its own privacy policy.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time: open the Profile tab, tap Privacy & Legal, then tap Delete My Account. This permanently removes the anonymous account and its data from our servers. Your favorites stay on your device and the app keeps working as before. (The app needs an anonymous ID to download content, so on the next launch it will create a fresh one — it still contains no personal information.)

If you cannot access the app, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous ID is not personal data and cannot be used to identify you.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before or at the time of the relevant app release, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com.
