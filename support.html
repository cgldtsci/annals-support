Annals: History & Its People

Annals is a reference app for exploring history through its people — and the civilizations and events they shaped. Browse historical figures, rulers, civilizations, and events on an interactive timeline. The full American collection is free; an optional subscription unlocks civilizations beyond America, with new content arriving over time. The app downloads its curated content from our servers when it opens, so it needs an internet connection to display its articles.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?
No sign-up is needed. The app automatically creates an anonymous account behind the scenes to download its content — you never enter a name, email, or password. You can delete it anytime from the Profile tab.
Does the app need an internet connection?
Yes. The app downloads its content from our servers each time it opens. No registration is required.
Is there a subscription?
The full American collection — figures, events, and civilizations — is free. An optional auto-renewing subscription (weekly, monthly, or yearly) unlocks civilizations beyond America, with new content arriving regularly. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions).
How do I restore a purchase?
Subscriptions are sold through the App Store. To restore, tap "Already subscribed? Restore" on the subscription screen, or use the Restore button on the subscription card in the Profile tab.
Which devices are supported?
Annals runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Privacy

Annals uses only an anonymous identifier to download content — no personal information. See the Privacy Policy for details.
