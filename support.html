Annals: History & Its People — Support

Annals is a free reference app for exploring history through its people and the civilizations and events they shaped. Browse historical figures, rulers, civilizations, and events on an interactive timeline — all offline, with content built into the app.

Need Help?

If you have questions, run into a problem, or want to send feedback, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?
No. Annals has no sign-up and no login — open it and start exploring.

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. All content is bundled with the app and works fully offline.

How do I restore a purchase?
Annals is free with no in-app purchases in this version, so there is nothing to restore.

Privacy

Annals collects no data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
