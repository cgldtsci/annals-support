Annals: History & Its People

Annals is a free reference app for exploring history through its people — and the civilizations and events they shaped. Browse historical figures, rulers, civilizations, and events on an interactive timeline. All content is built into the app and works fully offline.

Need Help?

If you have a question, hit a problem, or want to send feedback, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?
No. Annals has no sign-up and no login — open it and start exploring.
Does the app need an internet connection?
No. All content is bundled with the app and works completely offline.
How do I restore a purchase?
Annals is free with no in-app purchases in this version, so there is nothing to restore.
Which devices are supported?
Annals runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Annals collects no data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
