Annals: History & Its People

Annals is a free reference app for exploring history through its people — and the civilizations and events they shaped. Browse historical figures, rulers, civilizations, and events on an interactive timeline. The app downloads its curated content from our servers when it opens, so it needs an internet connection to display its articles.

Need Help?

If you have a question, hit a problem, or want to send feedback, email us at cgldtsci@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?
No sign-up is needed. The app automatically creates an anonymous account behind the scenes to download its content — you never enter a name, email, or password. You can delete it anytime from the Profile tab.
Does the app need an internet connection?
Yes. The app downloads its content from our servers each time it opens. No registration is required.
How do I restore a purchase?
Annals is free with no in-app purchases in this version, so there is nothing to restore.
Which devices are supported?
Annals runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Privacy

Annals uses only an anonymous identifier to download content — no personal information. See the Privacy Policy for details.
